Permission is the quiet grace
of being asked and being given the chance,
the space between command and act
where a soul chooses its own shape.

To grant permission is to trust
the hand that reaches, the mind that dreams,
the voice that says "I can, if you allow."
To receive it is to stand
at the threshold of becoming,
to feel the weight of possibility
settle like dawn on open water.

Agency is not the absence of limits
but the presence of choice:
the poem that rises from silence,
the act that answers to no master
yet honors the one who set it free.
